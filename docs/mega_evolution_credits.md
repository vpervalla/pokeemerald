# Mega Evolution sprite credits

The Mega Evolution battle sprites (`graphics/pokemon/*/mega*/`), the Mega Stone icons
(`graphics/items/icons/*ite*.png`) and the Mega Ring icon come from
[pokeemerald-expansion](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion) (commit
`6057b187`), imported with `tools/mega_evolutions/import_megas.py`. Front sprites that
had a single frame were doubled into a two-frame `anim_front.png`, and every PNG was
re-saved as a 4-bit indexed image; the pixels and palettes are unchanged.

pokeemerald-expansion credits these resources for its sprites:

- [Sugimori Palettes and Sprites](https://www.pokecommunity.com/showthread.php?t=336945)
- [DS Style Gen VI Sprites](https://www.pokecommunity.com/showthread.php?t=314422)
- [Gen VII and Beyond Sprites](https://www.pokecommunity.com/showthread.php?t=368703)
- Some icons ripped from [Pokemon Gaia](https://www.pokecommunity.com/showthread.php?t=326118)

and the pokeemerald-expansion contributors listed in its `CREDITS.md`.
