# Player costume sprite credits

## Kris (the Johto costume, girl)

Kris's overworld sprites (walking, running, bike, fishing, surfing, field move and VS SEEKER), her
trainer front and back pics, her intro picture and her map icon are **FourLeafSunny**'s
("Made by FourLeafSunny, give credit if used!"). They were imported unchanged, in the sheet's own
palettes, by `tools/costumes/import_kris.py`.

## Ethan (the Johto costume, boy)

Ethan's overworld sprites (walking, running, bike, surfing and field move) and his trainer front and
back pics come from an HGSS-style sprite sheet whose artist isn't named on it: add the credit here
if it's known. `tools/costumes/import_ethan.py` imported them. The sheet was a JPEG, so its colours
were brought down to 15 per palette and its background taken out, which leaves a few stray pixels.
His VS SEEKER frames were drawn over his field move, and his fishing frames are the old ones in the
new palette.
