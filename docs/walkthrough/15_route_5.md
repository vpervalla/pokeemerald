# 15 Route 5

**Arrival.** From Cerulean's south edge. **Cerulean's south exit is not open from the town centre:** the SLOWBRO lass and a Cut tree (26,32) close it. The way is **through the robbed house**: with the S.S. TICKET in the bag the policeman no longer blocks the door at (30,11); the house's back door (4,1) opens at (31,8) behind it. Walk east and south down the strip at x 39 to 40 to row 33, then the south corridors lead to Route 5. The thief ROCKET GRUNT at (33,6) (MACHOP L17, DROWZEE L17; ¥340) is on that side; he drops **TM28 (Dig)**. The Underground Path to Route 6 has [its own page](15b_underground_path_north_south.md).

## Map notes

- Route 5 is a road between fences with a Day Care (23,25) and the Underground Path entrance at (31,31). The grass strips between the roads are behind one-way ledges and fences: **not reachable on foot from either road** in this run (Meowth and the other grass Pokémon were caught on Route 6 instead).

## Bugs

None. (The Cut trees grow back whenever the map is reloaded; that is the original behaviour too.)
