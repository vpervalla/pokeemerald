# 12 Cerulean City

**Arrival.** East from Route 4 (Cerulean's left edge, offset 10) or Mt. Moon's east exit. Pokémon Center (22,19), Mart (29,28), Gym (31,21), the Route 24 bridge at the top (22,0). The buildings and the Gym have their own pages (Places below).

## Map notes

- **Moving around the town:** the town is split by one-way ledges and hedges. The **west plateau** (Center, the robbed house's front door (30,11), the rival's bridge) cannot be walked into the **east road** (x 35 to 47, Route 9, the strip at x 39-40 to Route 5): the hedge column at x=34 (y 12-17) and the ledge row at y=20 close it. They are joined only through the **robbed house** (see [Robbed house](12_cerulean_city--robbed_house.md)). The south exit is closed by a **Cut tree at (26,32)** (cut on the BICYCLE trip, [page 35](35_route_17.md)); otherwise Route 5 is reached from the east strip.
- Outside: a boy, a bald man ("I'll keep it at home, so it won't get dirty."), a woman, a youngster ("Collecting, battling… It's a tough life, isn't it?"), a SLOWBRO and its lass ("No! You blew it again!" / "Come on, SLOWBRO, pay attention!").
