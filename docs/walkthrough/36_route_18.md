## Story: DRATINI to L30

1. With all 19 Cycling Road trainers beaten DRATINI was L25 (21 901 of the 33 750 exp it needs: it grows slowly). You chose wild battles in Route 18's grass, with the Fuchsia Center next door.
2. **Solo first:** DRAGON RAGE only (40 a hit): DRATINI fainted to a RATICATE L25 and to DODUOs.
3. **Swap trick:** DRATINI out on the first turn, CHARIZARD in: safe, but half the exp, and the exp went to the strongest member. On the way **DRATINI reached L29 and learned SLAM in place of WRAP** (your choice).
4. **Your rule from here:** the Pokémon being trained fights as long as it has a real chance; otherwise it switches to the **lowest-level member that can still win** (SNORLAX L32, then WARTORTLE L34), never to an already strong one. The fight tool's `SWITCH=auto` does exactly this (switch when the opponent is more than 2 levels above or the trainee is at 30% HP, to the weakest healthy member at or above the opponent's level).
5. **DRATINI reached L30 and evolved into DRAGONAIR** (L30, Adamant, holding the DRAGON SCALE). SNORLAX gained exp on the way.
6. Healed at the Fuchsia Center and saved: `saves/ethan_dragonair.sav`.
