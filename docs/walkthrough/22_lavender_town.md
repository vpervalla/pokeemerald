# 22 Lavender Town

**Arrival.** From Route 10's south edge (8,79) into Lavender Town (map 36.4) at (8,1). The town is small: the Pokémon Center (6,5), the Volunteer Pokémon House (10,11), two houses (5,16) and (10,16), the Mart (20,15) and the **Pokémon Tower** (18,6). Each has its own page (Places below).

## Map notes

- **Outside:** the little girl ("Do you believe in ghosts?"), a worker, a boy; the Tower signs: "POKéMON TOWER: Becalm the Spirits of POKéMON", "New SILPH SCOPE! Make the Invisible Plain to See!".
- The south edge leads to Route 12, the west edge to Route 8.
