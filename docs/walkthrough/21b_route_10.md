# 21 Route 10

**Arrival.** From [Route 9](21_route_9.md)'s east end into Route 10's north part: the Pokémon Center (13,20) behind four Cut trees at x=10, the Power Plant doors (7,40) and (2,37), and [Rock Tunnel](21c_rock_tunnel.md)'s north door (8,19). The south part is reached through the tunnel and leads to Lavender Town.

Wild DAY: EKANS, SANDSHREW, SPEAROW, VOLTORB; NIGHT adds **GASTLY** (for the Gengar goal): not caught yet. - **Route 10 south (all done):** CAROL, CLARK, HERMAN, TRENT beaten (about ¥3 600), hidden NANAB BERRY (20,57). The south edge leads to **Lavender Town**: its Center was used to heal and save (`saves/ethan_lavender.sav`). Lavender's Tower, houses and shop are not visited yet.
