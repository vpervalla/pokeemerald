# 44.2 Cape Brink

**Arrival.** North of Two Island up the stairs (37,0); a small cape with a river, tall grass and a house.

## Map notes

- No trainers. Hidden **RARE CANDY** (15,13) and **PP MAX** (16,28) on a one-tile sand strip beside the river, reached by SURF.
- The house (12,16): the **starter-move tutor** ("I perfected the ultimate move of its type…") checks the lead: a fully evolved starter with maximum friendship learns FRENZY PLANT, BLAST BURN or HYDRO CANNON. All six of ours are at 255 friendship.

## Story

1. The RARE CANDY found (kept, not used).
2. **The PP MAX couldn't be picked up** from any side: FireRed marks it `underfoot` (found standing on it), which the port lost (bug 8). Fixed in the ROM (A with nothing in front checks the player's own tile), rebuilt, and from the Two Island save: surf up to (16,28), A: "ETHAN found one PP MAX!" The RARE CANDY was found again (the rebuild restarted from the save).
3. The tutor saw DRAGONAIR in front ("...No... I was just mistaken."). VENUSAUR, CHARIZARD and BLASTOISE all qualify: you chose **none for now**.
4. Saved: `saves/ethan_cape_brink.sav`.
