# 15 Underground Path (north to south)

**Arrival.** The entrance building at (31,31) on [Route 5](15_route_5.md); the south entrance comes out on [Route 6](16_route_6.md).

## Map notes

- The **Underground Path** (north entrance, long tunnel, south entrance) is a 60-tile corridor under Saffron with the colour of the walls cycling between olive, red and teal. Seven hidden items lie along it, all found with the face-and-press-A method: Antidote (5,6), Paralyze Heal (3,15), Awakening (1,24), Potion (5,30), Ether (3,39), Ice Heal (6,53), Burn Heal (2,57). The stairs at each end only work when you walk into them (LEFT at the south end, RIGHT to come back).
- The little girl at the north entrance: "People often lose things in the darkness of the UNDERGROUND PATH." The south entrance has a woman.
- The stairs: at the south entrance (7,4) walk LEFT into them, at the north end (4,3) walk RIGHT.

## Story

1. First crossing, from Route 5 to Route 6 on the way to Vermilion: the seven hidden items were found.
2. Crossed again on the FLASH trip (see [Rock Tunnel](21c_rock_tunnel.md)) and on the walk for the BICYCLE (see [Route 17](35_route_17.md)).
