# 33 Fuchsia City

**Arrival.** The west door of Route 15's gate ([page 32](32_route_15.md)) opens on Fuchsia City (map 36.7). Every building, the Safari Zone and the Gym have their own page (see Places below).

## Map notes

- **Fuchsia City** (map 36.7): the Pokémon Center (25,31), the Mart (11,15), the Safari Zone office (28,16), the Warden's house (33,31), three houses ((14,31), (19,31), (38,31)), the **Gym (9,32)** and the **Safari Zone gate (24,5)**. The town's people: a fat man, the SLOWPOKE, VOLTORB, CHANSEY, OMANYTE and KANGASKHAN pens, a youngster, a little boy, an old man, a bug catcher and a lass (talked to in the sweep). Cut trees at (30,10), (21,13), (32,16), (24,22).
- The **fenced yard** at (31..40,23..29) is reached only through **House 2's north door** (see [House 2](33_fuchsia_city--house_2.md)); the **hidden MAX REVIVE (33,26)** is there: stand at (32,26), face right and press A. The first try failed with "Too bad! The BAG is full..." (the Items pocket's 30 slots were full), so the NUGGETs and the STAR PIECE were sold at the Mart and two LEFTOVERS given to ALAKAZAM and CHARIZARD to make room; the second try worked.
- Fuchsia's south edge (x 20..28) leads to Route 19 ([page 39](39_route_19.md)); the west edge (0,20) to Route 18; the east edge to Route 15.
