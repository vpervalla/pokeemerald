# 23 Route 8

**Arrival.** From Lavender Town's west edge: the town's rows 5 to 9 are blocked by the rocky hill, but **(1,9)** walks out onto Route 8 (map 36.26) at its east end (69,9). The route runs 70 tiles west to the Saffron City gate. It has a Cut tree at (33,15) and another at (47,12) (they grow back whenever the map is reloaded: a visit to Lavender counts), the **[Underground Path](23b_underground_path_east_west.md)'s east entrance** at (13,4) and the **west gatehouse** at (7,10).

## Map notes

- The tall-grass field with the three hidden berries (x 36 to 46, y 10 to 15) is closed by fences: its way in is the Cut tree at (47,12), from (48,12) facing west. Cut trees do not show as passable to the route planner after they are cut: walk through them by hand.
- Wild (day): GROWLITHE L15 to 18, VULPIX L16, MEOWTH L18 and L20, PIDGEY L18 and L20, EKANS, SANDSHREW; night adds GASTLY L16 and HOOTHOOT. **GROWLITHE and VULPIX caught.**

## Everyone and every building

- **West gatehouse** (door (7,10)): the Saffron gate guard: "I'm on guard duty. Gee, I'm thirsty, though! Oh, wait there, the road's closed." Giving him a **TEA** (from Celadon) opens the way ("Huh? I can have this drink? ... You can go on through. I'll share this TEA with the others"). The player has no TEA yet, so Saffron is closed from this side.
- Route 8 has no other people than its trainers (nine singles, the TWINS ELI and ANNE as a double battle).

## Story

1. **The sweep.** From the east: JULIA (CLEFAIRY L22 x2), RICH (GROWLITHE and VULPIX L24), GLENN (GRIMER, MUK, GRIMER L22), the four trainers in the column at x=29: PAIGE (NIDORAN♀, NIDORINA L23), LESLIE (KOFFING L26), ANDREA (MEOWTH L24 x3), MEGAN (L19 x5), STAN (POLIWAG x2 and POLIWHIRL L22), AIDAN (VOLTORB, KOFFING, VOLTORB, MAGNEMITE L20), the TWINS **ELI & ANNE** (CLEFAIRY and JIGGLYPUFF L22, a **double battle**), the bikers RICARDO (KOFFING x2, GRIMER L23) and JAREN (GRIMER x2 L24): about ¥6 500 in all. The `clearmap` tool stalled in the twins' double battle (it fights one-on-one): it was played by hand.
2. **The double battle** needs two active POKéMON: DR VENKMAN (GENGAR) was in front and **GHOST moves do nothing to NORMAL types** (CLEFAIRY, JIGGLYPUFF): he was switched for CHARMELEON (FLAMETHROWER), WARTORTLE (BUBBLE; JIGGLYPUFF's SING put it to sleep) and the pair fell.
3. **The poison bill.** The GRIMER/MUK/KOFFING fights left several POKéMON **poisoned**: in this ROM a poisoned POKéMON **faints while walking** (ALAKAZAM did at 2 HP on the road; CHARMELEON at 19 HP was cured with an ANTIDOTE in time). A fight against JAREN's GRIMERs also went wrong because of the tool: an earlier PIDGEY battle had GENGAR use GHOST moves that cannot touch NORMAL/FLYING ("x0") until it fainted. Lesson: keep a lead whose moves hit the day's wild types.
4. **Hidden berries:** RAWST (42,10), LUM (38,11) and LEPPA (42,15), found with `hid` after cutting (47,12).
5. **Catches:** a **VULPIX L16** (PIKACHU's THUNDER WAVE, then GREAT BALLS; the ball loop kept pressing after the catch and typed a stray nickname, erased with B) and a **GROWLITHE L17** (VENUSAUR's SLEEP POWDER, then the `catch` tool: "Gotcha!"). Both are in Box 1.
6. IVYSAUR reached L32 and evolved into **VENUSAUR**; PIKACHU reached L33 and was offered AGILITY (declined: it keeps FLASH). The party was healed at the Lavender Center in between; the run is saved: `saves/ethan_route8.sav`.
