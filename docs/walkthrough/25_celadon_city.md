# 25 Route 7 and Celadon City (the streets)

**Arrival.** From Lavender by the **Underground Path**: Route 8's east entrance (13,4), the 76-tile east-west tunnel (seven hidden items, all found, see [Route 8](23_route_8.md)), the west entrance (a balding man: "I heard a sleepy POKéMON appeared out near CELADON CITY, too."), then **Route 7** (map 36.25): a short route with one **hidden WEPEAR BERRY (16,15)** (found), the Saffron gatehouse at (15,10) with the thirsty guard (he wants a TEA, as on Route 8) and **Celadon City** at its west edge (exit through x=0, y 1 to 3 to Celadon's east side (56,13)).
