**Arrival.** From Lavender by the **Underground Path**: Route 8's east entrance (13,4), the 76-tile east-west tunnel (seven hidden items, all found, see [Route 8](23_route_8.md)), the west entrance (a balding man: "I heard a sleepy POKéMON appeared out near CELADON CITY, too."), then **Route 7** (map 36.25): a short route with one **hidden WEPEAR BERRY (16,15)** (found), the Saffron gatehouse at (15,10) with the thirsty guard (he wants a TEA, as on Route 8) and **Celadon City** at its west edge (exit through x=0, y 1 to 3 to Celadon's east side (56,13)). ## Story

1. **From Lavender to Route 7** through the Underground Path, then the Center for healing and `saves/ethan_celadon.sav`.
2. **The street sweep** (`clearmap`): the **Leaf** battle was triggered by the sweep tool's "talk" but the fight tool did not start it the first time: Walking to (13,17) and pressing A facing up (she stands at (13,16)) starts it: "ETHAN! Fancy meeting you here!". **NIDORINA** (85 HP) fell to PSYBEAM; the lead GENGAR switched for ALAKAZAM, which at L33 **learned CALM MIND** (replacing DISABLE: the move-learn prompt has two text boxes before the YES/NO, then the move list: Disable is the third entry). The battle was won with ALAKAZAM, WARTORTLE (BUBBLE on PONYTA), VENUSAUR and PIKACHU; ¥2 000.
3. The Cut trees at (52,22) and (40,35) were cut from the side facing them; the south one opens the road to the Gym and the old man at (9,30) (his line: see above).
4. **The buildings** (all but the Game Corner and the Gym) were visited: Restaurant, House, Hotel, the Mansion with its roof and the **EEVEE**, and the Department Store with the roof vending machines (three TMs), then the Center's people. The **KEY ITEMS gained: COIN CASE and TEA** (the TEA opens the Saffron gate guards on Routes 5, 6, 7 and 8). The Game Corner and its hideout (the SILPH SCOPE) came next, see [page 26](26_game_corner_rocket_hideout.md); the Gym is left.

The west edge (0,21) of the city leads to **Route 16** (map 36.34). The Cut tree at (41,11) was cut. **SNORLAX L30 (139 HP, Quirky)** sits at (31,13) on the bridge: with the POKé FLUTE it woke and was caught (box 1) with the same plan as on Route 12: CHARIZARD (SMOKESCREEN then WING ATTACK, about 43 a hit, FLAMETHROWER only about 26), PIKACHU's THUNDER WAVE, VENKMAN's HYPNOSIS, and balls thrown while it slept after its REST (it sleeps two turns each time: two WING ATTACKS took it from 139 to about 55, then a GREAT BALL; four tries, the last one caught it).
