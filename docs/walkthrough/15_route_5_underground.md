# 15 Route 5 and the Underground Path

**Arrival.** From Cerulean's south edge. **Cerulean's south exit is not open from the town centre:** the SLOWBRO lass and a Cut tree (26,32) close it. The way is **through the robbed house**: with the S.S. TICKET in the bag the policeman no longer blocks the door at (30,11); the house's back door (4,1) opens at (31,8) behind it. Walk east and south down the strip at x 39 to 40 to row 33, then the south corridors lead to Route 5. The thief ROCKET GRUNT at (33,6) (MACHOP L17, DROWZEE L17; ¥340) is on that side; he drops **TM28 (Dig)**.

## Map notes

- Route 5 is a road between fences with a Day Care (23,25) and the Underground Path entrance at (31,31). The grass strips between the roads are behind one-way ledges and fences: **not reachable on foot from either road** in this run (Meowth and the other grass Pokémon were caught on Route 6 instead).
- The **Underground Path** (north entrance, long tunnel, south entrance) is a 60-tile corridor under Saffron with the colour of the walls cycling between olive, red and teal. Seven hidden items lie along it, all found with the face-and-press-A method: Antidote (5,6), Paralyze Heal (3,15), Awakening (1,24), Potion (5,30), Ether (3,39), Ice Heal (6,53), Burn Heal (2,57). The stairs at each end only work when you walk into them (LEFT at the south end, RIGHT to come back).

## Everyone and every building

- The little girl at the north entrance: "People often lose things in the darkness of the UNDERGROUND PATH." The south entrance has a woman.
- The house of the robbed man (30,11): the hiker ("Look what they've done to my house! They stole a TM for teaching POKéMON how to DIG holes! I was going to use it on a MANKEY or a SANDSHREW… That cost me a bundle, it did!") and later "I decided to teach DIGLETT how to DIG without a TM. I figure what's lost is lost." A girl: "TEAM ROCKET must be trying to DIG their way into no good!"

None. (The Cut trees grow back whenever the map is reloaded; that is the original behaviour too.)
