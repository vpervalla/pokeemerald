# 19 Vermilion Gym

**Arrival.** Door (14,25), south of the cut tree. Trainers: Engineer BAILY (VOLTORB L21, MAGNEMITE L21), Sailor DWAYNE (two PIKACHU L21), Gentleman TUCKER (PIKACHU L23) and LT. SURGE (VOLTORB L21, PIKACHU L18, RAICHU L24).

## Story

1. The gym guide (4,17): "LT. SURGE has a nickname. People refer to him as the Lightning American! He's an expert on electric POKéMON. BIRD/WATER-type POKéMON match poorly against the ELECTRIC type. He's locked himself in, so it won't be easy getting to him."
2. **The trash-can puzzle:** 15 cans in a 5x3 grid; the first switch was under the can at (1,12) ("Hey! There's a switch under the trash! Turn it on!"), the second was the neighbouring can at (3,12) ("The second electric lock opened! The motorized door opened!"). The other cans say "Nope! There's only trash here."
3. **MANKEY with DIG (TM28, Ground)** beat BAILY's VOLTORB/MAGNEMITE and SURGE's team alone (SURGE left it at 5/58 HP); CHARMELEON's EMBER finished BAILY's MAGNEMITE. Prize ¥2400, the THUNDER BADGE and TM34.
4. SURGE offers a **card duel** after the battle: the A mashing started one. The duel works (a deck of 15 cards, SPEAROW and RATTATA as active and bench, ENERGY cards, "Retreat", "Look at the cards", "Forfeit the duel? A: yes B: no"); forfeited.

## Bugs

None. (A card duel started by mashing A, as before.)
