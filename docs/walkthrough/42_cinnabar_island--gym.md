# 42.5 Cinnabar Gym (Blaine)

**Arrival.** Door (20,4), locked ("The door is locked...") until the SECRET KEY from the Pokémon Mansion.

## Map notes

- Six rooms joined by gates. Each gate has a **quiz machine** (read facing north): a right answer opens the gate; a **wrong answer brings that room's trainer over for a battle**, and beating them opens the gate too. Answering every machine wrongly is the way to fight all the trainers.
- Quizzes and their wrong answers: (22,10) "CATERPIE evolves into METAPOD?" NO; (15,2) "There are nine certified POKéMON LEAGUE BADGES?" YES; (13,10) "POLIWAG evolves three times?" YES; (13,17) "Are electric moves effective against GROUND-type POKéMON?" YES; (1,18) "POKéMON of the same kind and level are not identical?" NO; (1,10) "TM28 contains TOMBSTONY?" YES.
- The Gym guide (24,20): "The hotheaded BLAINE is a FIRE POKéMON pro. Douse his spirits with water! You'd better take some BURN HEALS, too."
- BLAINE (5,4): GROWLITHE, PONYTA, RAPIDASH, ARCANINE.

## Story

1. **Gym trainers, SNORLAX in front** (best move by type, no type switches): burglar QUINN (quiz 1), super nerd AVERY (quiz 2), burglar RAMON (quiz 3), super nerd DEREK (quiz 4), burglar DUSTY (quiz 5), super nerd ZAC (quiz 6) and super nerd ERIK (25,4), fought once the gates were open. SNORLAX reached **L55**.
2. **BLAINE**, BLASTOISE in front: four SURFs. "Hah! The VOLCANOBADGE heightens the SPECIAL stats of your POKéMON." **VOLCANO BADGE** (7th), **TM38 FIRE BLAST** ("Don't waste it on WATER POKéMON."), ¥4 700.
3. Then BLAINE's story: he was a researcher at the Mansion, where MEWTWO was made from MEW's genes; studying it they found **MEGA EVOLUTION** ("for one battle, and only with a trainer they trust"; a KEY STONE worn by the trainer and a MEGA STONE held by the POKéMON); MEWTWO broke loose and the MEGA STONES were lost in the fire. He gives the last KEY STONE: **the MEGA RING**. "In battle, press START when you choose a move. It will Mega Evolve before anyone moves that turn. Only once per battle."
4. Before the Gym, GENGAR's SHADOW PUNCH was replaced by **SHADOW BALL** by editing the save (your request; `tools/playthrough/save_edit.py move 1 325 247 15`), after the ROM was rebuilt with the FireRed/LeafGreen level-up learnsets (GENGAR now learns SHADOW BALL at L45, which it had passed).
