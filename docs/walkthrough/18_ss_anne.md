# 18 S.S. Anne

**Arrival.** Dock at (23,34) in Vermilion; the sailor checks the S.S. TICKET. The ship has 26 interior maps: the exterior, the 1F corridor and seven rooms, the kitchen, the 2F corridor and six rooms, the captain's office, the 3F corridor and the deck, the B1F corridor and five rooms. Doors on 1F open by stepping on the door tile and pressing the direction; the stairs only work by walking into them.

## Story

1. **1F:** rooms 2, 5 and 7 hold trainers (Lass ANN: PIDGEY L18, NIDORAN♀ L18; Youngster TYLER: NIDORAN♂ L21; Gentleman ARTHUR: two NIDORANs L19 for ¥1520; Gentleman THOMAS: two GROWLITHE L18). TM31 (Brick Break) lies in room 2.
2. **2F corridor:** **rival Blue** triggers at (30,6): PIDGEOTTO L19, RATTATA L16, KADABRA L18, WARTORTLE L20 (¥720). The first try went wrong: PIKACHU, MANKEY and CHARMELEON fainted, SLEEP POWDER from BUTTERFREE and the others finished it.
3. **2F rooms:** Fisherman DALE (GOLDEEN, TENTACOOL), Gentleman BROOKS (PIKACHU L23), a **double battle** against Gentleman LAMAR and Lass DAWN (GROWLITHE, PONYTA, RATTATA, PIKACHU: ¥1648 together, the double battle tool had to be written for it), an X ATTACK, STARDUST in room 2, and a GENTLEMAN, OLD MAN, WOMEN in the other rooms.
4. **Captain's office:** the captain is seasick ("Ooargh… I feel hideous… Urrp! Seasick…"): talk to him from the side (the trash can in front of him only says "Yuck! Shouldn't have looked!"), "Rubbed the CAPTAIN's back", and he gives **HM01 CUT**. Only CHARMELEON can learn it (the others say NOT ABLE).
5. **3F corridor and deck:** sailors TREVOR (MACHOP L17, TENTACOOL L17) and EDMOND (MACHOP L18, SHELLDER L18), and another sailor (¥576). A sailor at (10,13) and the deck sailors can trap you at the railing: walk around.
6. **B1F:** Fisherman BARNY, Sailors PHILLIP, HUEY, DYLAN (three HORSEA), a **double battle** with DUNCAN and LEONARD (SHELLDER L21), a hidden HYPER POTION at (21,5), **TM44 (Rest)**, an ETHER, a SUPER POTION.
7. **Kitchen:** seven chefs, a GREAT BALL at (1,10), hidden CHESTO, PECHA and CHERI berries (between the chefs: face them from the west).
8. The exterior's hidden LAVA COOKIE at (58,28) could not be reached.
