# 28 Saffron City (the streets)

**Arrival.** From Celadon east to Route 7, then the gatehouse (15,10): the thirsty guard drinks the **TEA** ("Huh? I can have this drink? Gee, thanks!... Glug, glug... Gulp... If you want to go to SAFFRON...") and lets you through; the east door (11,5)/(11,6) of the gatehouse leads to Saffron City (map 36.10) at (8,27). The gates on Routes 5, 6 and 8 open the same way. The buildings, Silph Co. and the Gym have their own pages (Places below).

## Map notes

- **Saffron is occupied by Team Rocket:** eight grunts stand in the streets, and **they block the doors** of the Copycat's house (22,14), the Gym (46,12) and the house (27,21) until the Silph Co. building (33,30) is cleared. The doors that work: the **Dojo (40,12)**, the **Mart (40,21)**, the **Pokémon Center (24,38)**, **Mr. Psychic's house (43,38)** and the **Pokémon Trainer Fan Club (47,21)**; the **Silph Co.** door (33,30).
- Door tiles: the Center's door is on the south face (stand at (24,39) and press up). The Fan Club's mat is a dead tile: step off and back on.
- The streets' Rockets only talk: "What do you want? Get lost!", "BOSS said he'll take this town in the name of TEAM ROCKET!", "Don't get defiant! Or I'll have to hurt you!", "SAFFRON belongs to TEAM ROCKET!", "My life as a criminal makes me feel so alive!", "We'll get stinking rich, yeahah!", "With SILPH under control, we can exploit POKéMON around the world!", and one asleep ("Hah! He's taking a snooze! ... Snore..."). A youngster ("Ow! Watch where you're walking!") and others add nothing.
