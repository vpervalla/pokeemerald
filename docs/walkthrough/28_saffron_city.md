## Map notes

- **Saffron is occupied by Team Rocket:** eight grunts stand in the streets, and **they block the doors** of the Copycat's house (22,14), the Gym (46,12) and the house (27,21) until the Silph Co. building (33,30) is cleared. The doors that work: the **Dojo (40,12)**, the **Mart (40,21)**, the **Pokémon Center (24,38)**, **Mr. Psychic's house (43,38)** and the **Pokémon Trainer Fan Club (47,21)**; the **Silph Co.** door (33,30) is the next page.
