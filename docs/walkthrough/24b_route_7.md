# 24 Route 7

**Arrival.** The west entrance of the [Underground Path](23b_underground_path_east_west.md) comes up on Route 7 (map 36.25), a short route between Celadon (west) and Saffron (east).

## Map notes

- A short route with one **hidden WEPEAR BERRY (16,15)** (found), the Saffron gatehouse at (15,10) with the thirsty guard (he wants a TEA, as on Route 8).
- The west exit to [Celadon City](25_celadon_city.md) is the strip at x=0, y 1 to 3 (Celadon's east side (56,13)); from the south part of the route it is reached through the ledge gap at x 6..7.
- Route 7's wild list: BELLSPROUT, GROWLITHE, MEOWTH, ODDISH, PIDGEY, VULPIX by day; at night MURKROW L19 and L22 (not caught yet) replaces PIDGEY.

## Story

1. Crossed from the Underground Path to Celadon; the hidden WEPEAR BERRY found.
2. Later the gatehouse guard drank the TEA from the Celadon Mansion and opened the way to [Saffron City](28_saffron_city.md).
