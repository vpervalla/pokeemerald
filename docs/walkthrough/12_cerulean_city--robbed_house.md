# 12.5 The robbed house

**Arrival.** Front door (30,11); back door inside at (4,1), out at (31,8).

## Map notes

- The policeman stands in front of the door: "The people here were robbed." The door is shut **until the S.S. TICKET is in the bag** (the map's transition script only blocks it while the flag is unset); then the house, its back door (4,1) and the strip behind it (the thief ROCKET GRUNT at (33,6), TM28 DIG) lead on to Route 5. Later the policeman: "It's obvious that TEAM ROCKET is behind this most heinous crime!"
- It is the only link between the west plateau and the east road: from the Center to Route 9, front door, through the house, back door, the east road to (47,18).
