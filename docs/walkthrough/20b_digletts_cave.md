# 20 Diglett's Cave

**Arrival.** The south entrance is the building at (6,7) at the west end of [Route 11](20_route_11.md); the north entrance comes out on [Route 2](05_route_2.md), north of Viridian.

## Map notes

- An old man at the south entrance and a hiker at the north; the cave floor is one big B1F with wild **DIGLETT** (L18) and **DUGTRIO** (L29 to L31). DIGLETT caught with a Gust and a ball. **DUGTRIO** L29 (62 HP): STUN SPORE, two GUSTS to 22 HP, and the first ball held (the first one met, L31, was knocked out by a last GUST: weaken with one move less). Diglett's Cave has no trainers.

## Story

1. First crossing: DIGLETT and DUGTRIO caught (see the map notes).
2. **Second crossing.** Walked again (south entrance (6,7) to the north entrance and back) for the Route 2 trip: the north exit is (3,3) on the B1F map, the south one (82,71). Wild DIGLETT L15-20 and DUGTRIO come every few steps; with the lead's moves chosen by type the crossing took about ten fights. Lesson: do not pass a slot number as `SLOT` to `goto` unless you know the slot's move (it once used FLASH in a fight); the default `TYPES=1 SWITCH=auto` is safer.
