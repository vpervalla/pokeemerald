# 17 Vermilion City

**Arrival.** South of Route 6 (offset -12), or the Underground Path. Center (15,6), Mart (29,17), Gym (14,25), the S.S. Anne dock (22 to 24,34), Pokémon Fan Club (12,17), houses at (9,6), (19,17) and (28,24). The buildings, the ship and the Gym have their own pages (Places below).

## Map notes

- **The dock sailor** checks the S.S. TICKET ("Welcome to the S.S. ANNE!").
- **The Gym** stands behind a **Cut tree at (19,24)**: the tree grows back whenever the map is reloaded (leaving through a door), so each trip to the Center or Gym needs a new CUT.
- Outdoor NPCs: an old man ("Did you see the S.S. ANNE moored in the harbor?"), a man with a MACHOP ("My POKéMON is tamping the land to lay the foundation."), a woman, a scientist. Several of the sweep sheets came back blank (the NPCs move): to repeat.
- The east edge leads to Route 11 (and back to Route 12 / Fuchsia).
