# 17 Vermilion City

**Arrival.** South of Route 6 (offset -12), or the Underground Path. Center (15,6), Mart (29,17), Gym (14,25), the S.S. Anne dock (22 to 24,34), Pokémon Fan Club (12,17), houses at (9,6), (19,17) and (28,24).

## Everyone and every building

- **Mart.** POKé BALL ¥200, SUPER POTION ¥700, ANTIDOTE, PARALYZE HEAL, AWAKENING, ICE HEAL, REPEL. The **first clerk visit of the day gives a free card pack** (5 cards, a HITMONLEE among them). 8 SUPER POTIONS bought (¥5600).
- **The dock sailor** checks the S.S. TICKET ("Welcome to the S.S. ANNE!").
- **The Gym** stands behind a **Cut tree at (19,24)**: the tree grows back whenever the map is reloaded (leaving through a door), so each trip to the Center or Gym needs a new CUT. Charmeleon is the Cut user.
- Outdoor NPCs: an old man ("Did you see the S.S. ANNE moored in the harbor?"), a man with a MACHOP ("My POKéMON is tamping the land to lay the foundation."), a woman, a scientist. Several of the sweep sheets came back blank (the NPCs move): to repeat.
- Unvisited: the Fan Club and three houses.
