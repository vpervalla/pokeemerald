1. **The way to the west half is the north lane, not the gatehouse's bottom room.** Route 16 is two lanes split by a picket fence: the south lane (Celadon, the SNORLAX bridge, the gatehouse's bottom room with its bike check) and the north lane (tall grass, the couple, the gatehouse's top room). They join through the Cut tree at **(41,11)**: stand at **(41,12) and cut it facing UP** (the game borrows a wild Pokémon: "The wild PARAS used CUT!"; nobody needs CUT).
