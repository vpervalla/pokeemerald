# 11 Route 4 (east)

**Arrival.** From Cerulean's west edge (the map connects at Route 4's (107,11)), or down the Mt. Moon B1F exit. The walkable part is a pocket of tall grass east of the ledges, x 75 to 107, y 10 to 18.

## Map notes

- The road is split in two by ledges. The lower grass pocket (where the party fought) is reached from Cerulean's west edge. The **upper corridor** (rows 3 to 8) holds LASS CRISSY at (75,3), a cooltrainer at (109,3), **TM05** at (67,5) and two black belts at (47,3) and (50,3) (move tutors: Mega Punch, Mega Kick). It could not be reached on foot from the pocket (a ledge only jumps south into it) and Cerulean's walled west side has no walkable link to it either: it probably needs CUT or another route. Left for later.
- Warps on the west half: Mt. Moon 1F (19,5), Mt. Moon B1F (32,5), the Pokémon Center at (12,5).

## Wild Pokémon (night)

RATTATA, SPEAROW, SANDSHREW, EKANS (L6 to L12). First-visit rule: **all four caught** (Spearow L10, Rattata L10 and Sandshrew L10 sit in the PC because the party is full; Ekans L6 was weakened with TACKLE to 5/21 HP and caught with the first ball). MANKEY was already owned.

## Story

1. Mart purchases at Cerulean: 12 POKé BALLS, a PREMIER BALL, 8 POTIONS (¥834 left).
2. About 25 encounters in the grass; BUTTERFREE did most of the fighting (L18 to L19); the first Ekans was lost to a CONFUSION that knocked it out in one hit (a weak move is needed to catch).
3. After each catch the nickname screen appears: mashing A types letters (an "AAA" had to be cleared with B), so use `aftercatch` (B, then START and A).
