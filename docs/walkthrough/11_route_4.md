# 11 Route 4

**Arrival.** The west part holds Mt. Moon's entrances and the Route 4 Pokémon Center (12,5) (see below); the east part is reached from Cerulean's west edge (the map connects at Route 4's (107,11)), or down the Mt. Moon B1F exit. The walkable part is a pocket of tall grass east of the ledges, x 75 to 107, y 10 to 18.

## Map notes

- The road is split by ledges into a lower grass pocket (x 75 to 107, y 10 to 18: reached from Cerulean's west edge, where the wild Pokémon were caught) and the **upper road** (rows 3 to 8), which is reached on foot from the Mt. Moon B1F exit at (32,5): TM05 at (67,5), the two move tutors (black belts at (47,3) Mega Punch and (50,3) Mega Kick) and LASS CRISSY at (75,3) are all up there. *Correction to an earlier note: the upper road is walkable, I had tested the planner from the wrong side.* Crissy's team in this build is PARAS L31 x2 and PARASECT L31 (¥496): a late-game trainer, to be fought when the party is stronger.
- Hidden items (not yet searched): a Great Ball at (43,2), a Persim Berry at (5,4), a Razz Berry at (67,17).
- Warps on the west half: Mt. Moon 1F (19,5), Mt. Moon B1F (32,5), the Pokémon Center at (12,5).

## Wild Pokémon (night)

RATTATA, SPEAROW, SANDSHREW, EKANS (L6 to L12). First-visit rule: **all four caught** (Spearow L10, Rattata L10 and Sandshrew L10 sit in the PC because the party is full; Ekans L6 was weakened with TACKLE to 5/21 HP and caught with the first ball). MANKEY was already owned.

## Story

1. Mart purchases at Cerulean: 12 POKé BALLS, a PREMIER BALL, 8 POTIONS (¥834 left).
2. About 25 encounters in the grass; BUTTERFREE did most of the fighting (L18 to L19); the first Ekans was lost to a CONFUSION that knocked it out in one hit (a weak move is needed to catch).
3. After each catch the nickname screen appears: mashing A types letters (an "AAA" had to be cleared with B), so use `aftercatch` (B, then START and A).
