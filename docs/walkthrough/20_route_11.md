## Everyone and every building

- **Trainers (all beaten):** Youngster EDDIE (EKANS L21), Gamer HUGO (POLIWAG, HORSEA L18), Youngster DILLON (SANDSHREW, ZUBAT L19), Youngster DAVE (NIDORAN♂, NIDORINO L18), Gamer JASPER (BELLSPROUT, ODDISH L18), Gamer DARIAN (GROWLITHE, VULPIX L18), Engineer BRAXTON (MAGNEMITE L21), Youngster YASU (two RATTATA, RATICATE L17), Gamer DIRK (VOLTORB, MAGNEMITE L18) and Engineer BERNIE (MAGNEMITE x2, MAGNETON L18). The ELECTRIC engineers killed four party members in the first try (a whole-team near-wipe: PIKACHU and MANKEY fainted to VOLTORB; BUTTERFREE, PIDGEOTTO and the others to BERNIE's three): CHARMELEON's EMBER (Fire against Steel) and ALAKAZAM's PSYBEAM carried it. Prizes about ¥6000 in total.
- **Items:** X DEFEND (42,12), GREAT BALL (63,13), AWAKENING (13,3) and a hidden ESCAPE ROPE (58,5).
- **Wild (day/night):** DROWZEE, EKANS, SANDSHREW, SPEAROW, HOOTHOOT. **DROWZEE** L11 caught: it has INSOMNIA (SLEEP POWDER fails), so one GUST and a ball did it.
