# 43 One Island (Sevii Islands)

**Arrival.** By the SEAGALLOP from Cinnabar with BILL, arriving on the dock (12,17).

## Story

1. "BILL: Here we are! This is ONE ISLAND. There are several islands around here, and this is one of them. My friend CELIO..." They walk to the Pokémon Center, where CELIO's machine can't link with BILL's PC ("we're too remote out here").
2. BILL stays to help and asks a favour: **the METEORITE** for a man on TWO ISLAND. CELIO gives the **TRI-PASS** (the ferry between ONE, TWO and THREE ISLAND) and the TOWN MAP gets an extra page.
3. Saved in the Center: `saves/ethan_one_island.sav`.
