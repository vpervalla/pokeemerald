# 22.4 The Name Rater

**Arrival.** Door (10,16).

## Map notes

- The NAME RATER: "Want me to rate the nicknames of your POKéMON?" YES, choose a POKéMON, "WARTORTLE, is it? That is a decent nickname! But, would you like me to give it a nicer name?" NO: "I see. Do come visit again." Only the original trainer's POKéMON can be renamed.

## Story

1. The Modest GASTLY's typo name (BBBCCCDDDE) was fixed here: an empty new name keeps the old one ("Done! From now on, this POKéMON shall be known as BBBCCCDDDE!"), and the on-screen keyboard's cursor wraps into the OK/BACK column, which made typing by key presses unreliable. A second, careful visit: the keyboard has three pages (SELECT cycles upper, lower, symbols), one key per press; typing **DR VENKMAN** worked ("Done! From now on, this POKéMON shall be known as DR VENKMAN!"). Nicknames hold 10 characters; the key at the top right of the letter grid enters a space, not a full stop.
