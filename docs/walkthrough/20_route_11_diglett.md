# 20 Route 11 and Diglett's Cave

**Arrival.** East of Vermilion City (its edge at (46,18)). The road has ten trainers and a gate in the east; Diglett's Cave's south entrance is the building at (6,7) in the west end of the route, and the cave leads to Route 2, north of Viridian.

## Everyone and every building

- **Trainers (all beaten):** Youngster EDDIE (EKANS L21), Gamer HUGO (POLIWAG, HORSEA L18), Youngster DILLON (SANDSHREW, ZUBAT L19), Youngster DAVE (NIDORAN♂, NIDORINO L18), Gamer JASPER (BELLSPROUT, ODDISH L18), Gamer DARIAN (GROWLITHE, VULPIX L18), Engineer BRAXTON (MAGNEMITE L21), Youngster YASU (two RATTATA, RATICATE L17), Gamer DIRK (VOLTORB, MAGNEMITE L18) and Engineer BERNIE (MAGNEMITE x2, MAGNETON L18). The ELECTRIC engineers killed four party members in the first try (a whole-team near-wipe: PIKACHU and MANKEY fainted to VOLTORB; BUTTERFREE, PIDGEOTTO and the others to BERNIE's three): CHARMELEON's EMBER (Fire against Steel) and ALAKAZAM's PSYBEAM carried it. Prizes about ¥6000 in total.
- **Items:** X DEFEND (42,12), GREAT BALL (63,13), AWAKENING (13,3) and a hidden ESCAPE ROPE (58,5).
- **Wild (day/night):** DROWZEE, EKANS, SANDSHREW, SPEAROW, HOOTHOOT. **DROWZEE** L11 caught: it has INSOMNIA (SLEEP POWDER fails), so one GUST and a ball did it.
- **Diglett's Cave:** an old man at the south entrance and a hiker at the north; the cave floor is one big B1F with wild **DIGLETT** (L18) and **DUGTRIO** (L29 to L31). DIGLETT caught with a Gust and a ball. **DUGTRIO** L29 (62 HP): STUN SPORE, two GUSTS to 22 HP, and the first ball held (the first one met, L31, was knocked out by a last GUST: weaken with one move less). Diglett's Cave has no trainers.

## Second crossing

Walked again (south entrance (6,7) to the north entrance and back) for the Route 2 trip: the north exit is (3,3) on the B1F map, the south one (82,71). Wild DIGLETT L15-20 and DUGTRIO come every few steps; with the lead's moves chosen by type the crossing took about ten fights. Lesson: do not pass a slot number as `SLOT` to `goto` unless you know the slot's move (it once used FLASH in a fight); the default `TYPES=1 SWITCH=auto` is safer.
