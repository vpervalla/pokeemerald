# 12.3 The SQUIRTLE house

**Arrival.** Door (17,11).

## Map notes

- A woman ("Using good BERRY POWDER, I can make any kind of medicine"), an old man, and a SQUIRTLE: "Its TRAINER left it behind on NUGGET BRIDGE after it lost a battle… if it truly loves its TRAINER, it would change its mind."

## Story

1. Talking while the party leader (BUTTERFREE) is friendly makes the SQUIRTLE come out: **"ETHAN received SQUIRTLE"**, a free third starter (to the PC: the party was full). Mashing A through the scene hits the nickname screen: B.
2. It was raised from L12 to L24 on Route 6 by swap-outs (direct leveling failed at L12 with 33 HP) and became the party's WARTORTLE (later BLASTOISE).
