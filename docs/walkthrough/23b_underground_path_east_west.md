# 23 Underground Path (east to west)

**Arrival.** The east entrance building (13,4) on [Route 8](23_route_8.md); the west entrance comes out on [Route 7](24b_route_7.md), next to Celadon.

## Map notes

- The east entrance (door (13,4)): a woman ("The DEPARTMENT STORE in CELADON has a great selection. Do you go there very often?"). Stairs at (7,4) lead to the **East-West tunnel** (76 tiles to the west entrance on Route 7 and so to Celadon), whose **seven hidden items** are all found: POTION (7,3), PARALYZ HEAL (17,5), AWAKENING (31,4), BURN HEAL (45,3), ETHER (55,2), ANTIDOTE (62,5), ICE HEAL (70,3). The tunnel has no trainers.
- The west entrance: a balding man ("I heard a sleepy POKéMON appeared out near CELADON CITY, too.").

## Story

1. First crossing, from Lavender to Celadon: the seven hidden items found with the face-and-press-A method.
