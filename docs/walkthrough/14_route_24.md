# 14 Route 24 (Nugget Bridge)

**Arrival.** North from Cerulean at (22,0). The bridge has six trainers; at its north end the road turns east into [Route 25](14b_route_25.md).

## Story

Bug Catcher (12,31) ¥160, Lass (10,28) ¥192, Youngster (12,25) ¥224, Lass (10,22) ¥256, Camper (12,19) ¥288, then the **Team Rocket recruiter** at (12,15): after his EKANS L15 fell (¥480) he gives a **NUGGET** ("Come on, think of the opportunity!" the join-Rocket offer is declined), and at (12,9) **LEAF** (the girl who took BULBASAUR from Oak's lab; the sprite shows her walking after the fight): she talks ("Sorry you got there too late!" is her post-battle line) and fights **NIDORAN♀ L13, CLEFAIRY L13 and BULBASAUR L15** (¥900). The fight starts the moment you talk to her, so heal first. **TM45 (Attract)** lies at (11,4).
CHARMANDER led the bridge and grew to CHARMELEON L16 on the way.
